The Horo Horo no Mi is a Paramecia-type Devil Fruit that allows the user to conjure ghosts, making the user a Ghost Human. It was eaten by Gill Bastar, and sometime after its reincarnation it was eaten by Perona.
The powers of the fruit were first demonstrated by one of Perona's ghosts spying on the Straw Hat Pirates, but it was not until much later that the fruit itself was named.
