The Ushi Ushi no Mi, Model: Giraffe is a Zoan-type Devil Fruit that allows its user to transform into a giraffe hybrid and a full giraffe at will, making the user a Giraffe Human. It was eaten by Kaku, who received it from Spandam. In One Piece episode A, an unknown New World pirate used this fruit, before he was defeated by Portgas D. Ace and finished off by Marshall D. Teach.
