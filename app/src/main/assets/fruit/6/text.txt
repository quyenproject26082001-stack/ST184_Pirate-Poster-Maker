The Supa Supa no Mi is a Paramecia-type Devil Fruit that enables the user's body to gain characteristics of a steel blade to attack opponents with, making the user a Full-Body Bladed Human. It was eaten by former Baroque Works agent and current Cross Guild member Daz Bonez, also known by his alias Mr. 1.
The fruit's powers were first seen being used by Daz to slice a wall he was about to hit, after he had been kicked by Mr. 2. The fruit's powers were fully revealed during his battle with Roronoa Zoro.
This fruit's abilities were duplicated in the Seraph S-Hawk, via a transfusion of Green Blood.
