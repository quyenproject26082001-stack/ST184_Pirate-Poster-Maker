The Uo Uo no Mi, Model: Seiryu is a Mythical Zoan-type Devil Fruit that allows its user to transform into a hybrid and full Azure Dragon at will. Belonging to the "strongest class of Devil Fruit", it was once a World Noble treasure before it was stolen by Charlotte Linlin and eaten by Kaidou during the God Valley Incident 38 years ago.
At some point, Dr. Vegapunk extracted a sample from a captive Kaidou's Lineage Factor and, with it, successfully created an artificial replica of this Devil Fruit. However, the given transformation was not of the same color as the original, and so solely for this reason the result was deemed a failure by Vegapunk, accidentally tricking the World Government into thinking the fruit was powerless. The replica was eventually consumed by Kouzuki Momonosuke, causing him to obtain a dragon transformation nigh-identical to that of Kaidou, save for its color.
