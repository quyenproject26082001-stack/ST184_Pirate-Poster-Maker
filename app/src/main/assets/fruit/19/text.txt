The Nikyu Nikyu no Mi is a Paramecia-type Devil Fruit that grants the ability to repel anything via paws permanently manifested on the user's palms, making the user a Paw Human. It was a treasure of the World Nobles before it was stolen and eaten by Bartholomew Kuma.
This fruit's abilities were duplicated in the Seraphim S-Bear, via a transfusion of Green Blood.
